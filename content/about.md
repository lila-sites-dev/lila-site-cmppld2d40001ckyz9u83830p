<div class="abt-prose">

We're dingg salon, a friendly hair salon in the heart of Pune. We're a neighbourhood spot at heart: somewhere you can drop in, talk through what you want, and leave feeling a little more like yourself. No rush, no script — just good hair and good company.

What makes us different is how we work, not how loud we are about it. Every head of hair is its own thing, so we start with a proper conversation — your routine, your texture, the look you're actually after — before anyone picks up the scissors. Our stylists love keeping up with the trends this city is so full of, and they love an easy, lived-in style just as much. Whatever you walk in for, the aim is the same: hair that suits you and feels like you.

We'd rather earn your trust one visit at a time than promise the world. Come in once, see how the chair feels, and we think you'll want to make us your regular spot in Pune.
