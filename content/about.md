<div class="abt-story">

We started dingg salon with a simple idea: good hair care should feel personal, not rushed. We're a hair salon in the heart of Pune, and we built the place around the kind of visit we'd want ourselves — an unhurried chat about what you're after, honest advice on what suits you, and a finish you can recreate at home.

No two heads of hair are the same, so we don't treat them that way. Whether it's a fresh cut, a colour you've been thinking about for a while, or a treatment to bring some life back, our stylists take the time to understand your hair and your routine before anyone picks up the scissors.

What keeps people coming back is the feel of the place. It's warm and easy-going, the work is done with care, and you're never just another appointment. We'd love for you to experience that for yourself.
