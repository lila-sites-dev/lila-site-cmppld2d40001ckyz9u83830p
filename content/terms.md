<h1>Terms & cancellation</h1>
<p>A little clarity goes a long way. Here is how appointments, changes, and no-shows work at dingg salon in Pune — kept simple so your visit stays relaxed from booking to blow-dry.</p>
</div>

<div class="t-section">
<h2>Cancellation</h2>
<p>Plans change, and that is completely fine. We just ask for a heads-up so we can offer your slot to someone else on the waitlist.</p>
<ul>
<li>Please cancel or reschedule at least <strong>24 hours</strong> before your appointment.</li>
<li>Cancellations made inside the 24-hour window may be treated as a late cancellation, in line with our no-show policy below.</li>
<li>The quickest way to change a booking is to reach us through the <a href="/contact">book your appointment now</a> page.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="t-section">
<h2>No-show</h2>
<p>When an appointment is missed without notice, your stylist's time is held empty for the full booking. To keep things fair for everyone:</p>
<ul>
<li>A missed appointment with no notice may be subject to a <strong>no-show fee of 50%</strong> of the booked service value.</li>
<li>For services that require a deposit, the deposit may be applied toward this fee.</li>
<li>We will always talk it through with you first — life happens, and we would rather get you rebooked.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="t-section">
<h2>Late arrival</h2>
<p>Pune traffic has a mind of its own, so a few minutes late is never a problem. For longer delays:</p>
<ul>
<li>Arriving more than <strong>15 minutes</strong> late may mean we shorten your service to protect the next guest's booking.</li>
<li>If there is not enough time left to do your service justice, we may ask to reschedule it.</li>
<li>Running behind? A quick message via the <a href="/contact">book your appointment now</a> page helps us hold things for you where we can.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="t-section">
<h2>Refunds</h2>
<p>Our stylists put real care into every service, and we want you walking out happy. Because salon services are personal and performed in the moment:</p>
<ul>
<li>Completed services are generally non-refundable, but your comfort matters most to us.</li>
<li>Retail products may be returned unopened and unused within a reasonable period of purchase.</li>
<li>If something is not sitting right, tell us — see the adjustment window in service care below.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="t-section">
<h2>Service care</h2>
<p>We stand behind the work our stylists do. If a finished service is not quite what you had in mind, we would love the chance to make it right.</p>
<ul>
<li>Let us know within <strong>7 days</strong> of your visit and we will book you a complimentary adjustment with your stylist.</li>
<li>Adjustments cover the service you originally booked — a fresh take, not a different look altogether.</li>
<li>Honest feedback only helps us look after you better next time.</li>
</ul>
</div>
