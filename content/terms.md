## Cancellation

Plans change, and that's completely fine. We just ask for **at least 24 hours' notice** if you
need to cancel or reschedule, so we can offer the slot to someone else and your stylist's day
stays on track. The easiest way to make a change is to get in touch ahead of time — see the
Contact section below.

## No-show

When an appointment is missed without notice, that chair time stays empty and your stylist
loses the booking. For missed appointments, or cancellations made with less than 24 hours'
notice, a **fee of 50% of the booked service price** may apply to your next visit.

## Late arrival

We hold your slot and want you to enjoy the full treatment, so please aim to arrive on time —
a few minutes early is even better for colour or chemical services. If you're running late,
let us know and we'll do our best to fit you in. Beyond around 15 minutes, we may need to
shorten your service to protect the following guest's booking, or rebook you for another day.

## Refunds

Salon services are personal and hands-on, so completed services aren't refundable. If you
booked but haven't yet been seen and cancelled within the window above, anything you've
pre-paid is fully credited toward a future visit. If something isn't right, please tell us — see
service touch-ups below.

## Service touch-ups

Your happiness with your hair matters to us. If you're not satisfied with a service, let your
stylist know within **7 days** and we'll arrange a complimentary touch-up of that service. This
covers adjustments to the work we did; it doesn't cover a change of mind to a different style or
issues from at-home colour or treatments applied after your visit.

## Contact

Questions about a booking, a change, or any of the above? Our team is happy to help — the
quickest way is to [book your appointment now](/contact), where you can also send us a note to
reschedule or ask about a policy.

You can also browse our [all salon &amp; spa services pune](/services) to plan your next visit.
