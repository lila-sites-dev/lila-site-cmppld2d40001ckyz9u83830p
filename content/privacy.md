# Privacy policy

This policy explains how Dingg Salon, a hair salon in Pune, handles the personal information you share with us when you visit this site or get in touch to book a visit. We keep things simple and only collect what we need to look after you well.

## What we collect

When you reach out through this site, you may share details such as your name, phone number, email address, and any notes about the service you're interested in. We also collect basic technical information that your browser sends automatically, such as your device type and how you navigate the site, so we can keep it working smoothly.

## How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange and confirm your appointment, and provide the service you asked for. We may also use it to keep records of your visit and to improve how this site and our salon work. We don't sell your personal information, and we only share it with the people and tools needed to run the salon and respond to you.

## Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use our pages. You can adjust or block cookies in your browser settings; if you do, some parts of the site may not work as smoothly.

## Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request that we correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make any of these requests, just contact us using the details below and we'll help you.

If you have any questions about this policy or about how we handle your information, please get in touch with **Dingg Salon** in Pune. The easiest way to reach us is through our contact page — [book your appointment now](/contact) and add a note, and we'll get back to you.
