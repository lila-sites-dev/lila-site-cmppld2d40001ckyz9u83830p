We collect information you choose to give us, such as your name and phone number

We use your information to respond to your enquiries, arrange and confirm

contact details to follow up about a booking. We do not sell your personal
information.

## Cookies

Our site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences
and understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies through
your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as intended if
cookies are turned off.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold
about you, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. To make any of these
requests, just reach out using the contact details below and we'll help.
