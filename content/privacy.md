## What we collect

When you get in touch or book a visit, we may collect the details you choose to give us — such as your name, contact details, and any notes about the service you're after. If you use a form on this site, we receive the information you enter there. We also collect basic, non-identifying usage data when you browse the site, as described under Cookies below.

## How we use it

We use your information to:

- Respond to your enquiries and arrange your appointments.
- Provide the salon service you've asked for and follow up about your visit.
- Keep a simple record of past visits so we can look after you better next time.
- Improve how this website and our service work.

We don't sell your personal information, and we only share it with the people and tools needed to run the salon and this website.

## Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working smoothly and to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use it. Most web browsers let you control or switch off cookies through their settings. Turning some cookies off may affect how parts of the site behave.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- Tell you what information we hold about you.
- Correct anything that's wrong or out of date.
- Delete your information, where we're not required to keep it.

To make any of these requests, just contact us using the details below and we'll be glad to help.

## How to contact us

This site is run by **dingg salon**, a hair salon based in Pune. If you have any questions about this privacy policy or about the information we hold, please [book your appointment now](/contact) or use the contact page to reach our team — we're happy to talk it through.
